Nutrition Month 2026 is a celebration of the importance of nutrition and its impact on our health and well-being. This year's theme, "Nutrisyon at Kalikasan, Ating Pangalagaan!" emphasizes the connection between nutrition and environmental sustainability. It encourages individuals to make healthier food choices that are also environmentally friendly, such as consuming locally sourced and organic foods, reducing food waste, and supporting sustainable farming practices. By promoting both personal health and environmental stewardship, Nutrition Month 2026 aims to create a healthier future for all.

Climate action means taking steps to protect the environment and address climate change. It includes reducing pollution, saving energy, using renewable energy, planting trees, and reducing waste. People, businesses, and governments all have an important role in protecting our planet. Climate action also helps communities prepare for problems caused by climate change, such as floods, droughts, and extreme heat. By working together and making responsible choices, we can reduce the effects of climate change and create a cleaner, safer future for everyone.

Sustainable Food Practices are important habits that help us take care of our planet and make sure we have enough food for the future.
This means choosing foods that are grown in ways that are good for the environment, like using less water and avoiding harmful chemicals.
It also means eating more plant-based foods, reducing food waste, and supporting local farmers. By making these choices, we can help protect nature, keep our food supply safe, and make sure everyone has access to healthy food for years to come.
